Method. Bayesian spectral matching (Zhong/Girolami-style analytic marginal likelihood): y = α·template + Legendre baseline + noise, Jeffreys prior, α/β/σ² integrated out analytically. Score = per-channel log Bayes factor vs baseline-only null. Tempered softmax posterior (κ=0.12, ECE 0.082 on VAST LOO), open-set threshold τ=0.552. Multi-modal fusion (v3): weighted sum of per-modality log BF (Raman 1.0, fluorescence 0.25, absorption 1.0). All computation runs in your browser — spectrum files never leave your machine.

Library: VAST (210 spectra, 34 classes) + RRUFF minerals + ROD + pharma (figshare) + NICODOM demo. Source code: github.com/SisypheanHUS/VAST
